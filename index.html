Returns & Cancellation Policy

Items purchased can be returned within **24 hours** of delivery. To be eligible, items must be unused, unblemished, and in original packaging. You must dispatch the package back to our official address.

Damaged Items (COD): If you receive a broken item, take a photo immediately at the time of delivery/unboxing and share it with us for instant replacement verification.

Shipping & Delivery

We ship across all major pin codes in India via trusted courier partners. Standard delivery takes 3 to 6 business days. Free shipping is applicable on all prepaid and COD orders above ₹999.

Privacy Policy & Security Notice

We collect your name, shipping address, pincode, and optional contact details solely to fulfill your orders and contact you regarding delivery updates.
